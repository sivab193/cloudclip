# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

People who move short-lived text—links, commands, notes, credentials, and ideas—between devices or share it with another person without wanting a heavyweight workspace.

Shared clips use a four-character uppercase alphanumeric code. The code or its short URL opens the clip; the client encrypts the text and the backend protects the recovery token with server-side encryption at rest.

## Product Purpose

CloudClip provides a private encrypted clipboard stream and expiring encrypted sharing links. Success means moving a useful fragment between contexts quickly while preserving user control and privacy.

## Positioning

CloudClip combines a lightweight personal clipboard stream with client-side encryption, region selection, and account-free expiring links. It is designed for continuity rather than permanent document storage.

## Operating Context

The web app is available now. Users choose an active geographic region before signing in or opening a shared clip. Native apps, encrypted file transfer, a custom CloudClip keyboard, and self-hosting are planned capabilities.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Text clips are supported today, including account-free encrypted sharing.
- Clipboard content is encrypted on the client before upload.
- Expiring encrypted links can be opened without an account using a short link or four-character code.
- Expiry choices range from 10 minutes to 7 days anonymously and 30 days for signed-in users; signed-in owners can change an active link's expiry.
- The US region is available today. The independent India deployment is coming soon.
- Files, background clipboard monitoring, native platform releases, custom keyboard integration, and end-user self-hosting are not yet available and must be labeled as coming soon.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is CloudClip. Its visual direction is calm and trustworthy: warm off-white and deep ink surfaces, a restrained violet action color, plain-language copy, and a simplified cloud/clip mark. Light and dark themes receive equal design attention. Product examples must be clearly labeled as examples.

## Evidence on Hand

The working application, regional deployment code, tests, and product logo are present in this repository. There are no customer testimonials, public performance benchmarks, or third-party audit claims to present.

## Product Principles

- Privacy and deployment claims must remain technically honest.
- The fastest useful path should stay visually and operationally obvious.
- Planned features must be distinguishable from working features.
- Regional choice is part of trust, not an infrastructure detail hidden from users.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Interactive controls require keyboard support, visible focus, meaningful labels, sufficient contrast, and touch targets suitable for mobile use.
